# ANTUTIVE GOOGLE PLAY AVAILABILITY UPDATE REPORT

**Date:** 11 September 2026
**Repository:** antutive website, branch `arshith` (working tree on top of commit `775a8d9a`)
**Scope:** present Famant as an available product that users get from Google Play; remove all pre-release, testing, gated-access and signup-dependent wording; introduce no launch date and no iOS distribution.
**State:** changes are in the working tree only. Nothing has been committed or deployed.

---

## Summary

Every public statement about Famant's availability on antutive.com now reduces to one message: **Famant is available on Google Play.** That sentence is a single shared constant and is used verbatim on the Home spotlight, the Famant page, the Technology intro and the Contact page; every other location uses a short variant of it ("Available on Google Play", "On Google Play"). Every action that previously pointed at an in-page "beta" section now links directly to the existing official Google Play listing, using the one store-URL constant the repository already had. Route metadata and the SoftwareApplication JSON-LD no longer carry a "beta" label. The legal pages no longer describe Famant as in beta, not yet generally available, or reached through signup or beta access.

The scroll-reveal / crawler implementation was not touched: `src/app/lib/scroll.tsx`, `src/main.tsx`, `src/entry-server.tsx`, `scripts/prerender.mjs`, `index.html`, `vercel.json` and everything under `public/` are unchanged. The production build succeeds, every prerendered file still ships all reveal wrappers visible, the complete site renders with scripts blocked, and the JavaScript-enabled reveal animation, client-side navigation and 404 behaviour were re-verified in a real browser session with no console errors.

## Remaining Manual Items

1. **Terms of Service wording (legal confirmation).** The scope sentence now reads "Famant, the product, is governed by its own product-specific terms, which are published separately from this website." This is the minimal replacement for the removed "in beta and not yet generally available; product-specific terms accompany beta access and will be published before general availability". Please confirm with counsel that product-specific Famant terms exist and are presented to users (for example inside the app or on Google Play); if not, adjust this sentence. The pages still carry their existing "interim version" banner dated 7 August 2026.
2. **Privacy Policy wording (legal confirmation).** The "Famant product data" section now says data submitted on famant.com or in the app "is governed by the notice presented there and inside the app". Confirm this matches the notices actually presented.
3. **Google Play link locale parameter.** The shared URL ends in `&hl=en_IN`. It works, but Google Play will show the listing in that locale; dropping the parameter would let it follow the visitor's locale. Left unchanged because the brief asks for the existing configured URL.
4. **Screenshot device framing (business decision).** The three phone captures remain in an iPhone-style frame while the site links only to Google Play. Alt text is platform-neutral. Left unchanged as instructed (preserve screenshots).
5. **Internal identifiers.** `FAMANT_ANDROID_BETA_URL`, `FAMANT_IOS_BETA_URL`, `androidBetaHref`, `PlatformBetaButtons` and the `.beta-store-*` CSS classes keep their historical names. They never render. A rename is optional housekeeping.
6. **Dormant iOS constant.** `FAMANT_IOS_BETA_URL` remains in `famant.ts` for internal reference and is confirmed absent from the production bundle.
7. **Tracked build artefact.** `dist/index.html` is tracked in git although `dist/` is ignored; it shows as modified after every build. Consider untracking it.
8. **Deployment.** Nothing has been committed or deployed. The live site still shows the previous wording until this branch is built and deployed.
